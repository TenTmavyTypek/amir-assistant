Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who uses Amir

Amir is a private, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

  • Google Calendar: Amir reads the owner's calendars to plan their schedule, and creates or changes events only in a separate calendar it created itself.
  • Notes and messages: what the owner tells Amir is stored in the owner's own notes on the owner's own server.

How data is used

Data is used only to provide the assistant to its owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Requests are processed by the AI model provider that powers the assistant, solely to generate its replies.

Google user data

Amir's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access to Google data can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
